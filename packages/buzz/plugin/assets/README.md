# Kybernesis cards

Your Kybernesis agent can answer with a card instead of a wall of text: a chart, a table, a few key numbers, a checklist, a short profile, or a page it made for you. This plugin draws those cards right in the conversation.

When the agent needs your yes, the question shows as what it would do: the email with its recipients and the whole message, the event, the purchase with its amount. Press Send it, Edit or Don't send, and your answer goes back as you, as a reply to the question. Forms show their fields, and other questions their choices. A request for a password or a card never takes it here: add it to your vault in KYBER Studio.

Without it, you still get the answer. The agent's reply always reads on its own, and each card has a text version.

## Pages

When the agent shares a page it made, such as a report or a dashboard, the page shows inside its card. Press Open to see it beside the conversation. Posting a page into a channel shares it with the people in that channel.

Pages show inside the card on the Mac app. Elsewhere you get the card and an Open button.

## Updates

Buzz doesn't update plugins on its own. When a new version is out, open Settings → Plugins, choose Kybernesis cards and press Update.
